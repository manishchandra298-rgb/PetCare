🐶 Dog Care Basics

Feeding schedules, exercise needs, and training basics for puppies through seniors.

Feeding

  • Puppies (under 6 months): 3–4 small meals a day.
  • Adult dogs: 2 meals a day, morning and evening.
  • Always keep fresh water available.
  • Avoid chocolate, onions, grapes, and xylitol — all are toxic to dogs.

Exercise

  • Most adult dogs need 30–60 minutes of activity daily.
  • Short, frequent walks work better for puppies and senior dogs.
  • Mental stimulation (puzzle toys, training) matters as much as physical exercise.

Training Basics

  • Start with simple commands: sit, stay, come.
  • Use positive reinforcement — treats and praise, not punishment.
  • Keep training sessions short (5–10 minutes) and consistent.
  • Socialize puppies early with people, other dogs, and new environments.
