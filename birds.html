🐦 Bird Care Basics

Cage size, diet variety, and social needs for common companion birds.

Cage Size

  • Choose the largest cage your space allows — width matters more than height for flight.
  • Bar spacing should be narrow enough that your bird's head cannot fit through.
  • Include several perches of different diameters for foot health.

Diet Variety

  • A quality pellet mix should form the base of the diet, not seeds alone.
  • Offer fresh vegetables and small amounts of fruit daily.
  • Avoid avocado, chocolate, caffeine, and salty foods — all are toxic to birds.

Social Needs

  • Most companion birds are highly social and need daily interaction.
  • Provide toys that encourage foraging and chewing to prevent boredom.
  • Watch for feather plucking or excessive screaming — often signs of stress or loneliness.
