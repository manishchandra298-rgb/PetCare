🐈 Cat Care Basics

Litter box setup, indoor enrichment, and reading your cat's body language.

Litter Box Setup

  • Keep one litter box per cat, plus one extra.
  • Scoop daily and fully change litter every 1–2 weeks.
  • Place boxes in quiet, low-traffic spots, away from food and water.

Indoor Enrichment

  • Provide scratching posts to protect furniture and support claw health.
  • Offer vertical space — cat trees or shelves — for climbing.
  • Rotate toys weekly to keep your cat interested.
  • Puzzle feeders encourage natural hunting behavior.

Reading Body Language

  • A slow blink is a sign of trust and affection.
  • A puffed tail or flattened ears usually signals fear or stress.
  • Purring can mean contentment, but also self-soothing during discomfort.
  • Tail flicking or thumping often means your cat wants space.
