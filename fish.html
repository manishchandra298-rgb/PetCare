🐟 Fish Care Basics

Tank cycling, water parameters, and stocking a tank without overcrowding it.

Tank Cycling

  • Run a new tank for 2–6 weeks before adding fish, to build beneficial bacteria.
  • Test ammonia, nitrite, and nitrate levels before introducing fish.
  • Add fish gradually rather than all at once.

Water Parameters

  • Check temperature, pH, and hardness regularly with a test kit.
  • Do a partial water change (10–20%) every 1–2 weeks.
  • Always treat tap water to remove chlorine before adding it to the tank.

Stocking a Tank

  • Follow a general guideline of about 1 inch of fish per gallon, adjusted for species.
  • Research adult size before buying — many fish outgrow small tanks quickly.
  • Avoid mixing aggressive and peaceful species in the same tank.
