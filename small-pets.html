🐹 Small Pet Care Basics

Rabbits, hamsters, and guinea pigs — housing, handling, and diet essentials.

Housing

  • Provide the largest enclosure possible — small pets need room to move and explore.
  • Use safe bedding (paper-based or aspen) — avoid cedar and pine shavings.
  • Clean the enclosure fully at least once a week.

Handling

  • Let your pet get used to your scent before attempting to pick it up.
  • Always support the body fully — never lift by the tail or ears.
  • Handle gently and briefly at first, building trust over time.

Diet

  • Rabbits and guinea pigs need constant access to fresh hay.
  • Guinea pigs require daily vitamin C — through diet or supplements.
  • Hamsters do well on a species-specific pellet mix with occasional fresh veg.
  • Introduce new foods slowly to avoid digestive upset.
